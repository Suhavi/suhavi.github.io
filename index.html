I am a computer scientist interested in understanding natural systems through computation. My work sits across machine learning and sensing solutions for ecological monitoring, climate science and decision-making under uncertainty.

I want to build tools that let us observe ecosystems better, understand how they are changing, and make better decisions about their future.
